Most GRC platforms were built as trackers, not operational systems. They log that work happened without doing anything to reduce the manual burden. Teams re-map the same controls every time a new framework enters scope, coordinate audit prep by hand across siloed departments, and get stuck flat-structuring compliance for an enterprise that’s anything but flat.
